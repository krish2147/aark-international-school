OUR CAMPUS

A campus designed for learning.

AARK’s low-rise campus is designed around natural light, air, open spaces and wide corridors. Its academic environment is supported by air-conditioned classrooms, technology-enabled learning spaces, robotics and AI labs, art and craft rooms, libraries and facilities for physical development.

Campus & Facilities

Spaces that support the whole school experience.

AARK’s official campus information describes infrastructure as an integral part of learning: spaces for academic exploration, reading, experiments, technology, creativity, movement and recreation, supported by qualified teaching professionals and continuing faculty enrichment.

01

Modern Classrooms

Air-conditioned, well-equipped and technology-enabled classrooms support everyday teaching and learning.

02

Library

A comprehensive collection of books and learning resources supports reading, research and independent exploration.

03

Science & Computer Labs

Laboratories support hands-on science work and technology education through practical learning.

04

AI & Robotics

Robotics labs and AI facilities form part of AARK’s technology-focused learning environment and futuristic approach.

05

Sports Facilities

Physical development is supported through sports facilities and activities including swimming and other age-appropriate sports.

06

Arts & Cultural Spaces

Dedicated arts and cultural spaces give students opportunities for creativity, self-expression, visual arts, music and performance.

07

Cafeteria

The school’s campus information includes a cafeteria providing meals for students during the school day.

08

Outdoor Spaces

Open-air areas, gardens and recreation spaces provide room for movement, relaxation and learning beyond classrooms.

09

Safety & Security

AARK states that student safety and well-being are a priority, with security measures intended to maintain a secure and comfortable learning environment.
