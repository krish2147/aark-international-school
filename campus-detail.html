OUR CAMPUS

A campus designed for learning.

AARK’s low-rise campus uses natural light, open-air spaces and wide corridors to create an inviting learning environment. Classrooms, laboratories, technology spaces, arts facilities, library resources and areas for physical activity support learning across the school day.

Campus & Facilities

Spaces for academics, creativity and movement.

The school’s published campus information highlights technology-enabled classrooms, library and laboratory spaces, AI and robotics facilities, arts areas, sports infrastructure and outdoor spaces designed to support academic and co-curricular learning.
