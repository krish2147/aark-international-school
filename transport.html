Transport.

How students travel to and from AARK, and what to know about the school's transport arrangements.

01

How Transport Works

AARK International School does not own or operate its buses or vans directly. The admissions team can provide contact details for the transport facilitator associated with the school. Families should confirm current routes, timings and charges directly with the facilitator before relying on them.

02

Requesting Transport

If your child needs transport, mention this when you enquire about admission or contact the school directly. The admissions office will connect you with the current transport facilitator's contact details.
