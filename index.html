Our Approach

More than a classroom.

Children do not grow in one direction. A good school gives them space to understand, ask, build, move, collaborate and become more confident in who they are.

At AARK, academic learning is complemented by technology, sports, creativity, values and experiences that support the development of the whole child.

The journey matters

Every year here leaves a mark on the chart.

A picture of progression — from early confidence and curiosity to deeper independence, responsibility and readiness for what comes next.

Each stage builds on the last, helping children develop greater understanding, independence and responsibility as they progress through school.

Beyond marks

A good education shows up in more than a report card.

Academic growth, physical development and character work together to shape a confident and capable learner.

01

Academic Growth

Understanding, curiosity, problem solving, learning habits and the confidence to think independently.

02

Physical Development

Movement, sport, physical confidence, discipline and healthy habits through regular participation.

03

Character & Confidence

Responsibility, empathy, collaboration, self-expression and the ability to contribute positively to a group.

FAQ

Quick answers before you call.

What grades does AARK offer?

AARK serves students from Pre-Nursery through Grade 12.

What curriculum does AARK follow?

AARK follows the CBSE curriculum.

Where is AARK located?

Nr. Shyam Icon, Bhayli-Sevasi Ring Road, Sevasi, Vadodara – 391101.

How can I apply for admission?

Start with an enquiry, visit or speak with the admissions team, then complete the application and admission formalities.

What documents are required?

Keep the student’s birth certificate, residential proof, medical certificate and a recent passport-size photograph ready. Admissions can confirm any additional grade-specific requirements.

Can I visit the campus?

Yes. Use the campus-visit enquiry below or call the school office.

Is transportation available?

Please contact the admissions team for the latest transport routes and availability.

What activities and facilities are available?

AARK offers learning experiences across technology, AI and robotics, swimming and sports, visual and performing arts, farming and outdoor activities.
