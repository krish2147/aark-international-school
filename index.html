Our Approach

More than a classroom.

Children do not grow in one direction. A good school gives them space to understand, ask, build, move, collaborate and become more confident in who they are.

At AARK, academic learning is complemented by technology, sports, creativity, values and experiences that support the development of the whole child.

What each stage builds

Each stage builds on the last, helping children develop greater understanding, independence and responsibility as they progress through school.

A child begins by feeling safe enough to participate, explore, communicate and build confidence in the rhythm of school.

Beyond marks

A good education shows up in more than a report card.

Academic growth, physical development and character work together to shape a confident and capable learner.

01

Academic Growth

Understanding, curiosity, problem solving, learning habits and the confidence to think independently.

02

Movement, sport, physical confidence, discipline and healthy habits through regular participation.

03

Responsibility, empathy, collaboration, self-expression and the ability to contribute positively to a group.
