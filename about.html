About AARK

Education for the whole child.

AARK International School follows global standards of the NEP and NCF curriculum. Its learning environment and study models aim to develop children linguistically, intellectually, physically, emotionally and socially while recognising individual potential. High-tech facilities, well-equipped labs and contemporary learning methods support the school’s focus on 360° development.

Vision

A happy, active and safe place to grow.

To provide a happy, active, safe and home-like atmosphere for children, while teaching important life values and helping students become responsible global citizens through quality education.

Mission

Academic excellence with character.

AARK’s mission is to nurture academic excellence and character development, preparing students for a dynamic future through a stimulating and positive environment that is safe, caring, loving, wholesome and happy.

Core Philosophy

How learning is approached.

  1. Provide stress-free education while reducing unnecessary school-bag weight.
  2. Focus on concept-based learning.
  3. Help children become independent physically, mentally, emotionally, linguistically and socially.
  4. Use concrete learning experiences involving different sensory and motor skills.
  5. Encourage and satisfy children’s curiosity.
  6. Use informal assessment and evaluation through play-way methods where appropriate.
  7. Provide regular recapitulation activities to strengthen lasting understanding and memory.

Quality Objectives

Preparing students for life beyond school.

  • Develop creative, compassionate and innovative young adults.
  • Achieve high standards of academic excellence.
  • Build knowledge, skills and attitudes required for later success.
  • Motivate students and stimulate an effective interest in learning.
  • Instil moral and ethical values, respect for diversity and empathy to develop responsible citizens.
