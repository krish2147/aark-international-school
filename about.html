About AARK

Welcome to AARK International School.

AARK International School follows global standards of the NEP and NCF curriculum, with a learning environment designed to develop children linguistically, intellectually, physically, emotionally and socially. The school combines academic learning with technology, sports, creativity, values and practical experiences to support holistic, 360° development.

Vision

A happy, active and safe place to grow.

To provide a happy, active, safe and home-like atmosphere for children, teach important life values, and help students become responsible global citizens through quality education.

Mission

Academic excellence with character.

To nurture academic excellence and character development through a stimulating and positive environment that is safe, passionate, caring, loving, wholesome and happy.
