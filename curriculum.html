Curriculum

A progressive journey from early foundations to senior school.

The curriculum follows CBSE requirements and is informed by NEP and NCF principles. Subject combinations, assessment details, and availability may vary by grade and academic session; the admissions team provides the current confirmed information.

Pre-Primary

Play, language, early numeracy, movement, sensory exploration, routines, and social-emotional development.

Primary

Languages, mathematics, environmental learning, arts, physical education, digital awareness, and integrated projects.

Middle School

Deeper subject learning, scientific inquiry, humanities, languages, technology, arts, sports, and skill development.

Secondary

Structured subject preparation, conceptual clarity, application, study skills, and guidance aligned with CBSE expectations.

Senior Secondary

Stream and subject choices are confirmed with families based on school availability and current board requirements.

Assessment

A balanced mix of ongoing feedback, classwork, projects, practical work, and formal assessment supports progress.

For the latest grade-wise subjects and assessment schedule, contact the academic office.
