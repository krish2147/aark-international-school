Curriculum

Learning that goes beyond textbooks.

AARK’s curriculum is presented around the global standards of the National Education Policy (NEP) and National Curriculum Framework (NCF), combining academic learning with technology, sports, creativity, personality development, innovation, values and practical experiences for holistic development.

Academic Excellence

Concepts, curiosity and application.

AARK emphasises academic excellence through contemporary teaching methods, well-equipped laboratories and technology-enabled learning. The approach aims to strengthen understanding, curiosity, problem solving and independent learning rather than limiting education to rote recall.

Sports Excellence

Movement matters.

Sports and physical activity support fitness, discipline, teamwork and confidence. AARK’s official program highlights activities including swimming, karate and skating.

Situational Training

Learning for real life.

Situational training is used to help students understand practical situations, respond appropriately and build useful life skills.

Farming

Hands-on understanding.

Farming activities introduce children to practical experiences connected with nature, food and the processes behind everyday life.

Musical Wall

Explore sound creatively.

The musical wall is part of AARK’s activity-based environment, giving children another playful way to explore sound and creative expression.

Adventure Zone

Confidence through activity.

The adventure zone brings physical challenge, exploration and recreation into the wider student experience.

Visual & Performing Arts

Space to imagine and express.

AARK includes visual and performing arts as part of holistic development, giving students opportunities in art, music, dance, drama and cultural activities that build creativity, expression and confidence.

Personality Development

Growing with confidence.

Personality development focuses on communication, confidence, social development and the broader qualities students need as they grow.

Innovation & Creativity

Think differently.

Innovation and creativity are encouraged through modern learning methods, technology and opportunities for students to explore ideas and create solutions.

Universal Values & Ethics

Character alongside knowledge.

The curriculum places importance on universal values and ethics, supporting empathy, responsibility, respect and responsible citizenship.

Beyond Academics

Co-curricular & extra-curricular life.

The school experience combines classroom learning with physical, cultural, creative and practical activities.
