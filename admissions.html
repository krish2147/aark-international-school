01

Admission Schedule

Academic Year 2026–27

Admissions are open from Nursery to Grade 12. Parents who prefer to complete the admission form at the school premises may visit during the published form-filling window.

School premises form timing: 8:00 AM to 1:00 PM.

02

Admission Criteria

Admission is subject to seat availability and the school’s admission policy. Current age criteria published for 2026–27 include:

  • Nursery — 3+ as on 31 May 2026
  • Prep Junior — 4+ as on 31 May 2026
  • Prep Senior — 5+ as on 31 May 2026
  • Grade 1 — 6+ as on 31 May 2026

For higher grades, contact the admissions team for the applicable age and eligibility requirements.

03

Admission Procedure

  1. Fill the official online admission form.
  2. Submit the required documents.
  3. Pay the non-refundable application processing fee of ₹1,000.
  4. The school reviews the application and schedules the interaction.
  5. The interaction schedule is communicated to parents by email.

04

Admission Instructions

  • Use a laptop or desktop to complete the online admission form.
  • All mandatory details must be completed.
  • Enter the child’s name exactly as shown on the Birth Certificate or Passport.
  • Enter the date of birth as shown on the official document.
  • Upload the requested student and parent photographs in the required format and size.
  • Check all information carefully before submission.
  • An incomplete form may not be considered.

Submitting the form does not itself confirm admission.

FAQ

Admission FAQs

Which grades are open for admission?

Admissions are open from Nursery to Grade 12 for the 2026–27 academic session, subject to seat availability.

What is the application processing fee?

The currently published application processing fee is ₹1,000 and is non-refundable.

Does submitting the online form confirm admission?

No. Submission is the beginning of the admission process and does not by itself confirm admission.

Are transport facilities available?

The school’s published FAQ states that the school does not own the buses or vans, but can help parents with contact details of the facilitator associated with the transport service.

Can I visit the campus before applying?

Yes. Parents are encouraged to book a campus visit or contact the admissions office.
