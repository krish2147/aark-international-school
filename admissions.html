Admission Schedule

Academic Year 2026–27

Admissions are open from Nursery to Grade 12. Availability can vary by grade, so families are encouraged to enquire early and confirm the current status with the admissions office.

Need assistance? Book a campus visit or contact the admissions team before beginning your application.

02

Admission Criteria

Admission is subject to seat availability and the school’s admission policy. Age eligibility applies according to the grade for which admission is requested.

For the latest grade-specific age criteria and eligibility requirements, please confirm with the admissions team before submitting the application.

03

Admission Procedure

  1. Enquire: Share your child’s details and the grade you are considering.
  2. Visit: Tour the campus or connect with the admissions team for counselling.
  3. Apply: Complete and submit the official admission form with the required information.
  4. Complete formalities: Follow the school’s guidance for documents, interaction and applicable fee formalities.

04

Before You Apply

  • Keep your child’s official name and date-of-birth details ready.
  • Have the required student and parent documents available before beginning the application.
  • Enter all mandatory information carefully and review it before submission.
  • Follow the upload requirements shown on the application portal.
  • Contact admissions if you are unsure about grade eligibility or required documents.

Submitting an application begins the admission process; admission remains subject to the school’s criteria and seat availability.

FAQ

Admission FAQs

Which grades are open for admission?

Admissions are open from Nursery to Grade 12 for the 2026–27 academic session, subject to seat availability.

Does submitting the online form confirm admission?

No. The application begins the admission process. Final admission depends on the school’s admission criteria, completion of required formalities and seat availability.

What documents should I keep ready?

The school’s published admission information includes the child’s birth certificate, residential proof, medical certificate and a recent passport-size photograph. Admissions can confirm any additional grade-specific requirements.

Are transport facilities available?

The school states that it can help families connect with the facilitator associated with the transport service. Contact admissions for current routes and availability.

Can I visit the campus before applying?

Yes. Families are encouraged to book a campus visit and experience the learning environment before making their decision.
