Admissions 2026–27

Begin your child’s journey at AARK.

Admissions for Nursery to Grade 12 are open for the 2026–27 academic session, subject to seat availability.

Apply for AdmissionBook a Campus Visit

01

Admission Schedule

Academic Year 2026–27. Admissions are open from Nursery to Grade 12, subject to seat availability. The school calendar records reopening on 6 April 2026 for Nursery through Grade 12.

02

Admission Criteria

  • Nursery: 3+ as on 31 May 2026
  • Prep Junior: 4+ as on 31 May 2026
  • Prep Senior: 5+ as on 31 May 2026
  • Grade 1: 6+ as on 31 May 2026

For other grades, confirm individual eligibility with the admissions team. Online admission facilities are available for Nursery to Grade 8; families seeking admission to Grades 9–12 should complete the procedure at the school.

03

Admission Procedure

  1. Complete the official online admission form.
  2. Submit required supporting documents.
  3. Pay the published ₹1,000 non-refundable application processing fee.
  4. The school reviews the application.
  5. Parents receive the interaction schedule.
  6. The admission result is communicated after the process is completed.

04

Before You Apply

  • Use the child’s name exactly as shown on the Birth Certificate or Passport.
  • Keep student and parent documents ready.
  • Review all mandatory information before submission.
  • Keep the payment receipt and required hard copies.

Submitting an application does not itself confirm admission.

FAQ

Admission FAQs

Which grades are open for admission?

Nursery to Grade 12 for 2026–27, subject to seat availability.

Does submitting the online form confirm admission?

No. Final admission depends on the school’s admission criteria, required formalities and seat availability.

What documents should I keep ready?

The published list includes the application form, birth certificate, Aadhaar documents, residential proof, immunisation details and previous-school records where applicable.

Are transport facilities available?

The school does not own the buses or vans. The admissions team can provide contact details for the transport facilitator associated with the school; confirm current routes and charges directly.

Can I visit the campus before applying?

Yes. Families can book a campus visit before applying.
