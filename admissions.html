Admission Schedule

Admissions are open from Nursery to Grade 12, subject to seat availability. The school also accepts admission-form enquiries at the campus; families should confirm current timings with the admissions office before visiting.

Need assistance? Book a campus visit or contact the admissions team before beginning your application.

02

Admission Criteria

Admission is subject to seat availability and the school’s admission policy.

  • Nursery: 3+ as on 31 May 2026
  • Prep Junior: 4+ as on 31 May 2026
  • Prep Senior: 5+ as on 31 May 2026
  • Grade 1: 6+ as on 31 May 2026

For other grades and individual eligibility questions, please confirm with the admissions team before submitting the application.

03

Admission Procedure

  1. Apply: Complete the official online admission form.
  2. Documents: Submit the required supporting documents.
  3. Processing fee: Pay the published ₹1,000 non-refundable application processing fee.
  4. Review: The school reviews the application before scheduling an interaction.
  5. Interaction: Parents receive the interaction schedule by email.
  6. Decision: The final admission result is communicated by email.

For Grades 9–12, the school’s published process asks families to complete the online procedure at the school.

04

Before You Apply

  • Use the child’s name exactly as shown on the Birth Certificate or Passport.
  • Keep the required student and parent documents ready before beginning the application.
  • Complete all mandatory information carefully and review it before submission.
  • Follow the photograph and document upload requirements shown on the application portal.
  • Keep your payment receipt and bring the required hard copies when requested by the school.

Submitting the application does not by itself confirm admission.

FAQ

Admission FAQs

Which grades are open for admission?

Admissions are open from Nursery to Grade 12 for the 2026–27 academic session, subject to seat availability.

Does submitting the online form confirm admission?

No. The application begins the admission process. Final admission depends on the school’s admission criteria, completion of required formalities and seat availability.

What documents should I keep ready?

The published document list includes the completed application form, birth certificate, Aadhaar documents, residential proof, immunisation details and previous-school records where applicable. Additional documents apply to some grades and circumstances.

Are transport facilities available?

The school states that it can help families connect with the facilitator associated with the transport service. Contact admissions for current routes and availability.

Can I visit the campus before applying?

Yes. Families can book a campus visit and experience the learning environment before proceeding with admission.
