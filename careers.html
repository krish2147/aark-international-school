How to Apply

Apply to join AARK.

Teachers and staff are welcome to apply at any time. Send your details below, then email your CV to info@theaarkinternational.com with the role in the subject line.

1. Send your detailsTell us the role you are interested in and a little about your experience.
2. Email your CVAttach your CV and any certificates to an email to info@theaarkinternational.com.
3. HR reviewThe HR team reviews every application and contacts shortlisted candidates about the next steps.
