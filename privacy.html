Privacy Notice.

How AARK International School uses the details you share through this website. Last updated October 2026.

Who we are

This website is run by AARK International School, Nr. Shyam Icon, Bhayli–Sevasi Ring Road, Sevasi, Vadodara, Gujarat 391101, managed by Daffodils Education Trust. For any question about your data, email info@theaarkinternational.com or call +91 99091 15550.

What we collect

  • Campus visit and admission enquiry forms: your name, phone number, the grade you are interested in, and, if you choose, your child's name and your email address.
  • Job applications: your name, phone number, email, the role you are applying for and anything you write about your experience. CVs you email to us are handled by the school's HR team.
  • Sahay, the website assistant: your questions are answered in your browser from the school's published information. The conversation is kept only in your browser for this visit and is not sent to the school.
  • Visit statistics: anonymous page-view counts (which pages are visited, roughly from which country and device type). These do not use cookies and cannot identify you.

Why we use it

Only to respond to your request: to call you about a campus visit or admission, to send a confirmation email if you gave an address, or to review your job application. We do not sell your details, use them for unrelated marketing, or share them with anyone outside the school except the service providers below.

Where it is kept

Form details are emailed to the school's office inbox and stored securely in the school's website admin, which only authorised school staff can open. The website is hosted by Vercel and email is sent through Google (Gmail). Spam protection may use Cloudflare Turnstile.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for up to two academic years so the admissions team can follow up, then deleted. Job applications are kept for up to one year. You can ask for deletion sooner at any time.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask to see, correct or delete the details you gave us, or withdraw your consent. Email info@theaarkinternational.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Children's information

Enquiry forms are meant for parents and guardians. We ask only for a child's name and grade, and only so we can arrange admission. Photographs of students on this website are published by the school for school purposes; to ask for a photo to be removed, contact the school office.
